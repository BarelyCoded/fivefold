================================================================================
FIVEFOLD - PATCH NOTES
================================================================================


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE OVERLAND COMES ALIVE (Shandalar alignment)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The world map was mostly static scenery. It is now a living overland with
roaming threats, escape routes, and a ticking clock.

* Roaming pursuit AI
  Enemies notice you from a distance (tougher foes see farther) and give chase,
  stepping toward you each move. Cities, mana links, landmarks, and castles are
  safe havens they will not step onto.

* Roads let you escape
  The dirt roads linking cities are now mechanical: stay on one and pursuing
  mages lose your trail and fall back to idle wandering, so you outpace them.
  The map panel flags when you are on a road.

* Five Warden guild castles
  One dark fortress per color region, each held by a Warden built from that
  color's roster plus two bombs. The Usurper hides behind one of them - storm
  the guilds to find and defeat the true enemy and win.

* Town sieges + a losing clock
  The surviving Wardens march on the free cities. A siege escalates 1/3 -> 2/3
  -> the city falls; let four cities fall and the realm collapses (its own loss
  ending). Walking into a besieged or captured city breaks the siege and
  reclaims it. Clearing a Warden's guild takes them off the board and eases the
  pressure.

* Mana motes
  Collectible sparks scatter across the map and keep re-seeding as you travel.
  Rewards now vary: gold, an amulet, a cache of provisions, or a rich vein worth
  both. And about 14% are ambush lures - a region-appropriate foe springs out
  (Duel / Bribe / Back away).

* A world-magic menu
  Spend amulets from anywhere on the map to bend the world (heal, blink, cloak
  from pursuit, and more).

* Power Nine from dungeon vaults
  The nine most powerful artifacts are no longer buyable; they are earned only
  by clearing dungeons.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SMARTER AI DUELIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Multi-blocker combat awareness
  The AI now evaluates gang-blocks and bad trades properly (it will not throw a
  3/3 into two 2/1 first-strikers, etc.) and uses combat abilities like Angelic
  Page and Vampire Bats.

* No more nonsense attacks
  It will not swing a defensive creature (e.g. a 1/4) into a bigger blocker for
  a pointless chip, and will not attack with 0-power creatures.

* Bounce valued as tempo
  It treats unsummon/bounce as a tempo play, not free removal, so it no longer
  bounces your beneficial-ETB creature (Sage Owl) on turn 2 for no reason.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CARDS & RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Animated manlands
  Mishra's Factory and friends can now animate and attack, reverting at end of
  turn, with summoning sickness respected.

* Cleaner shops
  Engine-unsupported and do-nothing cards (including Bronze Tablet) no longer
  appear in shops or amulet purchases.

* Power-tier ranking
  An in-game Alpha-Alliances power-tier browser you can open and re-rank on the
  fly, with card costs scaled by tier - handy for live balance tweaks.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUALITY OF LIFE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Mana symbols instead of letters in costs.
* Deck builder: add-all / remove-all, plus a mana curve chart.
* Auto-pass toggle and duel controls relocated under the turn-order list.
* Click a land to tap it for mana directly - no confirmation menu.
* Usurper mana links raised from 3 to 5.
